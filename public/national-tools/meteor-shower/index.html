What this tool knows—and what it doesn't

Inputs and transparency

  • NWS sky-cover grid: U.S. forecast data, checked for age and hourly coverage.
  • International Meteor Organization: annual shower windows, radiant reference and ideal ZHR.
  • Global Meteor Network: actual observed meteor flux, linked separately until machine ingestion is validated.

Reading the results

The 0–100 observing index compares weather and sky conditions; it is neither the probability of seeing a meteor nor a meteor-per-hour estimate. No forecast means no score. Seasonal peak dates outside 2026 are approximate until refreshed against the current IMO calendar. Nearshore clouds can vary sharply from the NWS grid forecast.

Watching meteors requires an unobstructed view of the sky. Dress for the weather, avoid unsafe roadside stops, and give your eyes 20–30 minutes to adapt to darkness.
