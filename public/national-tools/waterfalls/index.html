    Method

    Several signals, one hydrologic decision

    The waterfall is first attached to the USGS hydrologic network. The tool then looks along that network for a relevant streamgage and upstream regulation, retrieves local NOAA National Water Model guidance, compares flow to same-season USGS history, and reads NWS precipitation as supporting context. If the seasonal evidence is not good enough, the numerical spectacle score is withheld instead of invented.

    National Water Model values are guidance rather than an official NWS river forecast. Waterfall Window does not determine trail status, safe viewing distance, safe crossings, swimming conditions or access closures.
